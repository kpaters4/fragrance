Wheel vs. data
Where do they agree?

The classic Fragrance Wheel sorts every fragrance into one of 14 named subfamilies by its notes. The map's "emerged clusters" instead group fragrances by how their notes actually sit near each other, with no knowledge of the wheel at all. Each ribbon below is real fragrances flowing from a wheel subfamily (left) into an emerged cluster (right) -- a subfamily that funnels into one or two thick ribbons is a real, data-backed neighborhood; one that fans out thin across many is mostly a shared-keyword label, not a neighborhood the data agrees with.
